Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

NoContextCentral Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the NoContextCentral YouTube channel. It is used only by its operator to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

The app requests access to the operator's own YouTube account through Google OAuth, limited to uploading videos and reading basic channel information (the channel name). It does not access any other user's data.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the operator's own computer and is used solely to upload videos and confirm which channel is signed in. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
